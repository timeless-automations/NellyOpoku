The problem

Missed calls are missed customers

You can't be on the phone and do your job at the same time. A caller who doesn't get through may simply try the next business on their list.

Busy with a customer

Under a car, on a test drive or at a viewing. The phone rings and nobody can pick up.

Evenings and weekends

Many people only have time to call after work, exactly when your business is closed.

Voicemail and callbacks

Messages pile up, callbacks get forgotten, and a warm lead quietly goes cold.

Lena picks up every one of those calls and makes sure you never lose track of who called and what they need.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I keep my own phone number?

Yes. You keep your number and forward calls to Lena. You decide when: all the time, after hours, or only when you can't pick up.

Which languages does Lena speak?

Lena speaks Dutch, French and English with your customers.

What if Lena doesn't know an answer?

She doesn't guess. Lena takes a message and a callback request, and you're notified so you can follow up yourself.

Doesn't an AI receptionist sound robotic?

Lena talks in a natural, human-sounding voice, without “press 1, press 2” menus. Don't take our word for it: create your Lena and give her a test call.

How long does setup take?

You can create and test your Lena with this short onboarding. Going live is a matter of setting up call forwarding and connecting WhatsApp, and we help you with that.

Does it work with my current tools?

Yes. Lena works on top of what you already use. You keep your phone number and your way of working, and see calls, leads and appointments in the Timeless X dashboard.
